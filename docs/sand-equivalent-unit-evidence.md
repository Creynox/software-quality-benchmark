# Sand-equivalent unit evidence — 2026-10-14

## Primary source

Available uploaded document: DIN EN 933-8:2015-07, in the local BLE norm library.
No licensed PDF or substantive norm text is committed. Section 8.5, printed page
14 (PDF page 16), was extracted and visually checked. Heights h1 and h2 are
recorded to the nearest millimetre; section 6.5 specifies a 500-mm metric ruler.
Section 9 reports a whole-number mean of the two individually calculated ratios.

Public corroboration: [DIN Media edition](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-933-8/234010113)
and [CONTROLS EN 933-8 apparatus](https://controls-group.com/product/sand-equivalent-test-set/).
The public catalog is not the evidence for the exact measurement clause.

## What is not proved

The historical number 12.2 could represent a centimetre recording convention
or the value entered into an mm-labelled field. Neither the field name nor the
resulting ratio proves its actual physical scale. Scaling both heights by ten
leaves the percentage unchanged. A raw measurement record or instrument-scale
comparison is needed before converting these source values.

Current source-unit status: UNCONFIRMED. Conversion authorized: false. Original
